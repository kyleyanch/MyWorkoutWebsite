Common questions

Do I need a full gym?

No. Equipment chips on every exercise show what you need, and plenty of the library works with just dumbbells, a bar, or bodyweight. Mix and match with what you have.

How do I know how much weight to use?

Start light, master the movement, and add small increments only when you hit the top of the rep range with clean form. Comfortably hard is the target.

What do beginner and intermediate mean here?

Beginner exercises are easy to learn and low-risk. Intermediate exercises need more technique and care — read the safety note on each card before loading up.
