Training tips

Curl without swinging

Pin your elbows, lift with control, and lower slowly. Swinging turns a curl into a momentum exercise and wastes the effort.
Finish triceps reps

Fully extend the elbow at the top of pushdowns and extensions. That last half inch of range is where the triceps actually work.
Brace your core

Isometric holds like planks build stability where it counts. A short, tight hold beats a long, wobbly one.
High frequency is fine

Small muscles recover quickly. Train arms two to three times per week and add a little core work to every session.

Common questions

Should I train arms before big lifts?

No — compound lifts come first. Arm isolation at the end of a session still builds size without stealing energy from presses and pulls.

How many arm sets per week?

Eight to twelve quality sets per muscle across two to three sessions is a solid starting point. Add volume slowly.

Can I train abs every day?

Yes, but keep some days sub-maximal. Planks, rollouts, and hanging raises respond well to frequent, well-executed work.
